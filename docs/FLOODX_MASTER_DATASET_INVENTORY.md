- **Project:** FLOOD-X — AI-Powered Dynamic Flood-to-Action Decision Intelligence Platform

- **Audit Date:** 6 November 2026
- **Auditor Role:** Lead Technical, Data, ML & Security Systems Auditor
- **Status Classification Standard:**
  - `AVAILABLE`: Present in repository, validated, and directly ingested/utilized.
  - `AVAILABLE_BUT_NOT_INTEGRATED`: Physically present in repository, but not wired to operational services or APIs.
  - `PARTIALLY_AVAILABLE`: Available with documented geographic, temporal, or spatial resolution gaps.
  - `SIMULATED`: Synthesized, parametric, or illustrative benchmark data; not primary empirical telemetry.
  - `UNVERIFIED`: Real-world origin claimed but lacking statutory gazette or engineering documentation.
  - `MISSING`: Required for the end-to-end objective but absent from repository and environment.

## 2. Summary of Missing and Deficient Datasets

To achieve the complete operational objective (**PREDICT → ASSESS → PRIORITIZE → ROUTE → ALLOCATE → EXPLAIN → RE-PLAN**), the following data acquisitions remain necessary:

1. **Maharashtra WRD Khadakwasla Dam Telemetry Feed:**
   - *Current Status:* `MISSING`.
   - *Consequence:* The prospective riverine risk assessment engine cannot operate in autonomous live mode; it must force `DATA_INSUFFICIENT` and rely on manual What-If scenario parameter inputs.
2. **Official Gridded Population Raster (WorldPop 100m or Census 2011 Ward Polygons):**
   - *Current Status:* `MISSING`.
   - *Consequence:* Population exposure metrics are withheld (`null`) to prevent false precision. Accurate evacuation shelter demand cannot be calculated from physical population densities.
3. **Copernicus Sentinel-1 Level-1 GRD Raw Satellite Rasters:**
   - *Current Status:* `MISSING` (`data/raw/satellite/` directory is absent).
   - *Consequence:* The flood extent layer is restricted to an illustrative vector analog rather than scientifically reproducible SAR backscatter difference ($\Delta \sigma^0$) thresholding.
4. **Verified Municipal Shelter Register with Certified Capacities:**
   - *Current Status:* `UNVERIFIED` (64 records, all with `NULL` capacities).
   - *Consequence:* Evacuation destination recommendations cannot route citizens to certified shelters; the engine returns `NO_VERIFIED_SHELTER_AVAILABLE` and lists candidate facilities for emergency officer field reconnaissance only.
5. **Real Municipal Resource Register:**
   - *Current Status:* `SIMULATED` (23 seeded demonstration records).
   - *Consequence:* OR-Tools resource allocation operates exclusively in demonstration/simulation mode.
6. **In-situ Soil Moisture Sensor Probes:**
   - *Current Status:* `MISSING` (replaced by numerical NWP topsoil moisture reanalysis).
7. **Hydraulic Rating Curves & 1D/2D Hydrodynamic Flood Inundation Models (HEC-RAS):**
   - *Current Status:* `MISSING` (bridge thresholds are operational rules of thumb or illustrative scenarios).
